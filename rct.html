About Parallax Cube Timer

Parallax Cube Timer is an app designed to time rubik's cube solves, automaticly track personal records and calculate averages. As a beginner speedcuber and amateur programmer, I thought it would be a good idea to combine my 2 passions in a way that benifits them both. Whilst making this app, I learned how to save data which improves my games and the app itself will help me get better at speedcubing. Currently, I can only times solves and calculate averages but I plan on adding more soon.
