About tiles

Tiles is a simple mobile game about placing tiles in a grid to defeat the opponent. There are 4 tiles, attack, trap, flip and blank. The attack tile moves forward once per turn and destroys any tile in its way. when it reaches the other side, it moves the meter across by 1. The trap tile destroys attack tiles that are in front of it but it also gets destroyed. The flip tile will rotate attack tiles in front of it, sending it back to the enemy. The blank tile does nothing. When the meter reaches the side, someone wins the game.
