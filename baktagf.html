About Bring a knife to a gun fight

Dash 'n' Drive is a retro style arcade game. You control a car speeding down a highway and you need to dodge other cars using W and S or the arrow keys. You have 5 lives but you can get more by collecting them as they spawn (they look like small plus signs). Glitched cars take 2 lives and glitched lives make you immune for 1 hit.
